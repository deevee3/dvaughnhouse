SOP-008 · Research Training Requirements

Status: DRAFT v0.1 · 2026-10-10 · Not binding until approved Owner: Managers (training records) · Approver: President

Purpose

An SOP nobody was trained on doesn't exist. Nobody touches regulated research work — human subjects, clinical data, or anything under GCP — without current, documented training. Training is a gate, not a checkbox.

Scope

Every Glenride team member, contractor, and collaborator whose work touches research. Requirements scale with the role: everyone gets the foundations; regulated work gets the full stack.

Procedure

  1. Foundations (everyone). Before starting work: this SOP library (the SOPs that touch the person's role), SOP-005 (data integrity), and the conflicts-of-interest SOP. Recorded before the first task begins.

  2. Human-subjects work. Anyone involved in the design, conduct, review, or analysis of human-subjects research completes CITI Program training (or equivalent) in the relevant track — biomedical or social-behavioral — before assignment. Refresher every three years, or sooner if the institution or IRB requires it. Lapsed training means reassignment off the work until current.

  3. Clinical trial work. Anyone touching interventional trial design, conduct, monitoring, or data handling completes Good Clinical Practice training aligned to ICH E6(R3) (adopted January 2025) before assignment, refreshed every three years. CITI's GCP modules or equivalent are acceptable; the content must reflect R3, not the retired R2 framework.

  4. Role-based additions. Managers assign supplementary training where the work demands it — e.g., data-security handling for identifiable data (see the data-security SOP), adverse-event reporting for safety work. The Manager documents what was assigned and why.

  5. Records. Managers keep a training log per team member: course/program, completion date, expiry date, and certificate or transcript on file. The log is reviewed quarterly — expiries get flagged 60 days out, not discovered after lapse.

  6. Retraining on change. When an SOP is revised, everyone working under it is retrained on the changes before the new version takes effect. The revision doesn't bind until the training is recorded.

Records

  • Training log with completion dates, expiry dates, and certificates — kept by Managers, auditable by Operations.

Review

Annually, or when CITI, ICH, or regulatory training expectations change.

Basis

CITI Program standard curriculum structure; ICH E6(R3) training expectations; standard quality-system training practice (an untrained SOP is not implemented).
