# SOP-005 · Data Integrity — ALCOA+ Principles

**Status:** DRAFT v0.1 · 2026-10-10 · Not binding until approved
**Owner:** Every team member (Managers verify) · **Approver:** President

## Purpose

Data that cannot be trusted is worse than no data. This SOP sets the minimum bar for trustworthy research data at Glenride: every data point must satisfy the ALCOA+ principles recognized by FDA, EMA, and MHRA. If a regulator, partner, or auditor cannot reconstruct what happened from the record, the record failed.

## Scope

All research data generated, collected, processed, or held under the Glenride name — paper and electronic, primary and derived, including AI-assisted analysis outputs.

## Procedure

1. **Apply all nine principles to every record.** No exceptions, no "we'll fix it later."
   - **Attributable** — every entry shows who did it and when. No anonymous data entry, ever.
   - **Legible** — readable for the full retention period. No faded printouts, no cryptic shorthand without a key.
   - **Contemporaneous** — recorded at the time the work is done, not reconstructed days later. Back-dating is fabrication.
   - **Original** — the first recording is the record. Transcriptions are copies and must be labeled as such, with the original retained.
   - **Accurate** — truthful and valid. No editing results to fit expectations.
   - **Complete** — the whole record, including failed runs, outliers, and repeats. Deleting inconvenient data is falsification (see SOP-002).
   - **Consistent** — chronological order, no gaps, no contradictions between related records.
   - **Enduring** — recorded on a medium that survives the retention period. Sticky notes are not a medium.
   - **Available** — retrievable for review and audit for the full retention period.

2. **Corrections.** Never obscure the original. Paper: single line through the error, initial, date, reason. Electronic: use the system's correction function so the audit trail shows original value, new value, who, when, and why. Correction without a reason is not a correction — it's an edit.

3. **Electronic systems.** Every system holding regulated data must enforce: unique user logins (no shared accounts), automatic audit trails that cannot be edited by users, and timestamped entries. This mirrors 21 CFR Part 11 expectations for electronic records and signatures.

4. **Manager verification.** Managers spot-check records against ALCOA+ monthly for active studies. Findings go in the study record with corrective actions and dates.

5. **AI-assisted work.** Outputs from AI tools used in analysis are derived data: the prompt, model/version, date, and human review decision are part of the record. An AI output nobody reviewed is not a result.

## Records

- Study records with audit trails, retained per the data-retention SOP.
- Manager verification notes and corrective actions.

## Review

Annually, or after any data-integrity finding — then the procedure gets revised from what the finding taught.

## Basis

FDA guidance on data integrity; ICH E6(R3) Good Clinical Practice (adopted January 2025); MHRA data-integrity guidance.
